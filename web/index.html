NotifyVault

Keep a local snapshot of notifications before they disappear from your notification shade. NotifyVault records what Android delivers to your device and keeps the history available locally.

Download APK View source
Notification captureUses Android Notification Access with explicit user permission.
Local historyNotification snapshots are stored on the device by default.
Deleted-from-shade trackingRecords when an observed notification is later removed.

Important: NotifyVault cannot recover a message that was deleted before Android delivered its notification. The APK is a test/distribution build signed with the Android debug key.
